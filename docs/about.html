About

Who we are

LOOO is a small, independent shoe shop based in Gaborone, built around finding a good pair for everyday life. We bring together sneakers, slides, and reliable classics from brands our customers know and love. Our team takes time to help you choose a style, fit, and color that feels right without making the process complicated. From daily errands to weekends around the city, we look for footwear that feels comfortable, expressive, and ready to be worn. We are proud to serve our local community with thoughtful picks, friendly service, and a collection shaped by the people who shop with us.

How we operate

Getting your next pair is simple. Choose the way that works best for you.

01

Visit us in person

Come by our store in Gaborone to explore the collection, find your fit, and purchase your pair in person.

02

Order through the website

Choose a pair, select Add to cart or Buy now, and complete your contact details. Let us know whether you prefer delivery or an in-store collection.

Your order details are sent to us on WhatsApp. We’ll connect with you to confirm the next steps and stay in touch until your pair is in your hands.
